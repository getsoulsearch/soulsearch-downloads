Responsible use & privacy

SoulSearch is a free tool. Here is what it does, what it talks to, and what stays on your computer.

Using it responsibly_□x
  • SoulSearch downloads through Soulseek, a peer-to-peer network, from files other people have chosen to share.
  • You are responsible for what you download and for having the right to use it. Support the artists you play out: buy their music.
  • SoulSearch isn't set up to share any folders, so it shares nothing from your computer back on Soulseek.
What the app connects to_□x
  • GitHub and this website, to check for updates and download them. The one-click installs of slskd and ffmpeg also download from GitHub (and from gyan.dev for ffmpeg on Windows).
  • Soulseek, to search for and download your tracks.
  • Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and Apple Music, only to read the lists you point it at. If you leave the last-choice SoundCloud / YouTube option on, it also downloads from SoundCloud or YouTube when Soulseek doesn't have a track.
  • MixesDB, to look up tracklists for sets when you crate dig.
  • Discogs, only on the Automatic crate-dig route, to look up an artist's releases.
  • Anthropic, only if you choose the Automatic crate-dig route with your own Claude. The free route is copy and paste: you take the brief to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini yourself, and SoulSearch never talks to them.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no account with us.
What stays on your computer_□x

Your settings, your lists, your downloads and your keys stay on your computer. Passwords and keys (your Spotify secret, your Soulseek login) are kept in your computer's secure store: Windows Credential Manager, or the Mac Keychain.

This website_□x

The fonts are served from this site, so opening a page makes no request to Google or any other company. The only outside request is to GitHub, for download details when they aren't already on this site. A few things go further only when you press them: the download buttons go to GitHub, Send on the Contact page goes through Web3Forms (a form-to-email service), and the tip links go to Ko-fi or show a crypto address. No cookies, no analytics.
