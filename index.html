SoulSearch is free. It syncs your liked songs and playlists from Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube and Apple Music (Shazam too) into Rekordbox. Every new like or updated playlist is searched for on Soulseek and downloaded in the file type and quality you pick, then filed into your Rekordbox folder. Reload the list in Rekordbox and your tracks are ready to play.

It can also dig crates. Type a DJ or artist and a free AI (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, by copy and paste) researches the sets and mixes they have played. You check the tracklist, then SoulSearch downloads it into a folder of its own in Rekordbox.

The first time you open it

Windows
  • Your browser may say the file isn't commonly downloaded. Choose Keep.
  • If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  • A Windows Firewall box may ask about slskd. Allow it on Private networks.
Mac
  • Unzip it and drag SoulSearch into Applications. Open it from there, not from Downloads, or updates won't work.
  • Right-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
  • Still blocked? System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway (macOS 15 and newer).
  • A Keychain box may ask for permission. Choose Always Allow.

Full steps are in the setup guide. The app isn't signed yet, which is why your computer is cautious.
